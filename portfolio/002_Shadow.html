Project Overview

Platformer 3D puzzle game with two 2D view.

Tasks Completed

  • I've completed creating the 3D collision system for the player and objects.
  • I made two different 2D camera views and made most of the player movement logic.

Reflection

When faced with the variable of planning delays during a project, I seized the opportunity to enhance system flexibility.

By proactively building a data-driven puzzle system, I prepared for the frequent revisions that might otherwise have been required once the planning phase was finalized.

In particular-even after completing my own assigned tasks-I proposed and developed a communication tool that allowed for the immediate incorporation of design intentions, thereby helping to elevate the overall quality of the team's deliverables.

This experience served as a catalyst for me to deeply reflect on the specific technical support and communication strategies essential for seamless collaboration as a developer, and to translate those insights into practical, actionable solutions.
